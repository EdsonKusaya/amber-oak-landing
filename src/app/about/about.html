In the shop — Kingston, NY

Two hands, one workshop

Amber & Oak started in a rented garage in 2014 with a single bandsaw and more ambition than orders. Today it's still small on purpose: every piece that leaves the shop has been touched by one of us, start to finish.

We mill our own lumber from regional sawyers, join everything with traditional mortise-and-tenon or dovetail joinery, and finish by hand with hardwax oil rather than sprayed lacquer. It takes longer. It also means the piece can be repaired in twenty years instead of replaced.
